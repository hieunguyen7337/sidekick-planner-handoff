## Ambiguities resolved in code

- **holm_family_incomplete**: B2's convention: Holm is computed only when all five are complete, because a smaller family would change every adjusted p. Until then every complete L reads 'none (Holm family incomplete)' and the incomplete one reads 'incomplete'.
- **gate_incomplete**: The gate is a paired contrast and follows §3: below 114 non-crashed pairs it is INCOMPLETE, every L reads 'none (informativeness gate not evaluable: C_p - E incomplete)', and the planner does not count as passing for the overall claim.
- **gate_estimator**: The paired mean over the 114 (task_id, seed) keys (reported with its scenario and task intervals). The decision is on a point, not a bound, so POOL-04 does not apply. A point within 1e-09 of zero (float noise) counts as zero.
- **point_on_predicted_side**: As B2: every 'supported' / 'replicates' / 'reversed' also needs the point estimate on the predicted side of the threshold. With a percentile interval this binds only in a degenerate case.
- **l1_not_resolved**: 'not_resolved'. For the overall claim it is a reading that does not replicate (as are fails_to_replicate and reversed).
- **overall_claim_scope**: 'not_registered_case' with the reason whenever a planner is not analysed (--planners), fewer than two pass the gate, or a passing planner's L1 is incomplete, on_the_boundary or unread. L1 of a planner that fails the gate is never consulted.
- **pool04_decision_bearing_bounds**: L1: both bounds against 0 (three-way, A1's P6 rule). L2: the upper bound against 0. L3, L5: the lower bound against 0. L4: the upper bound against +7.00 pp. The seven re-draws are classified on the unadjusted interval, as j10.a1_pool04 does for A1 and B2. The gate has no bound.
- **signflip_direction**: The contrast's own p side, at its own threshold: L1 two-sided at 0; L2 'less' at 0; L3, L5 'greater' at 0; L4 'less' at +7.00 pp; the gate 'greater' at 0 (informational). 19 scenario clusters: exact enumeration of 2^19 patterns.
- **planner_strength_luna**: C_P8, C_P27 and luna's ceiling hj13_planner_alone_cap81_20260923 [prereg:64], each on the 57 x {1, 2} matrix. Descriptive: no direction, no verdict.
- **delta_scope**: B2's exploratory convention: Δ_p is estimated only when T_p, A_p, T_luna and A_luna are each complete and share all 114 keys; otherwise counts only.
- **m9_arms**: They are loaded and inventoried (counts, means) only.
- **missing_goal_pass_rate**: As B2: it is never coerced to 0; the pair is dropped and counted, which leaves the contrast below 114 and so INCOMPLETE.
- **duplicate_within_one_campaign**: As B2: a protocol error (exit 2), since keeping either copy would be a choice.
- **analysis_code**: The J10 A1 functions B2 uses; j10.cluster_bootstrap_means is draw-for-draw j8_frontier.paired_diff_scenario's algorithm (its docstring), at §3's settings.
