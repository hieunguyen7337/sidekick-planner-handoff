### Fix to report (not made)

`j10_report.py` is a J10 matrix script. It will not emit
`sft_plan − executor_alone` or a goal-pass contrast even when those two arms
are complete. If J8 staging (or any two-arm look) is supposed to reuse it
without a helper:

1. either document that J8a is out of scope and keep a thin wrapper that calls
   `paired_diff(resample="task")` on the inventoried `cleaned` cells, or
2. add an explicit partial-matrix mode that inventories whatever `--arm`s are
   given and emits pairwise contrasts **including** `goal_pass_rate`, without
   fabricating `sidekick`.

Do **not** satisfy (2) by aliasing `sft_plan` as `sidekick`. That is the A12
stand-in trick and would mislabel the contrast.

## 4 · What not to conclude

A +27 pp TGC gap (CI excluding 0) shows that **giving the trained executor a
cached one-shot plan, versus no plan, moves TGC a lot on this dev split.** It
does not show that adaptive allocation beats a fixed timer. That is what the
live J8 arms test, and they have not run.

These are **dev** numbers. Dev has been looked at for HJ-1R, J3, J4b, J6, and
A12 plumbing. They bound and they calibrate. They are not a test-split result
and they are not a thesis result.
